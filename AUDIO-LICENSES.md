# Audio sources and permissions

## Music

Music by [Purrple Cat][artist]. The repository owner identified the artist;
the 13 retained tracks were checked against the artist's Bandcamp pages on
November 4, 2026. Each page names Purrple Cat as the sole performing artist
and links to [CC BY-SA 4.0][license]. Those pages are the license evidence
used for this distribution, even where other release sites offer CC BY 4.0.

The recordings in `public/audio/music/` remain under CC BY-SA 4.0, including
the AAC format conversions. The source-code MIT license does not apply to
them. The full license is in [LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt][license-file].
Copying and redistribution, including commercial use, are permitted subject
to attribution and the license terms. Adaptations must use the same or a
compatible license. This does not relicense the application source code.

The files were already encoded as AAC. Exact original download URLs and
conversion history were not retained. Artist identification is based on
the owner's confirmation and the listed release pages, not embedded tags.
No endorsement by Purrple Cat is implied.

The player shows the artist and license, and links to [track credits][credits].
Redistributors must preserve the credits, source links, license notices,
and any modification notices, and must not impose additional restrictions.

## Sound effect

`public/audio/effects/switch.aac` is retained unchanged at the owner's
request. The owner reports obtaining it from a publicly accessible source.
Its source, author, and license remain undocumented. Public availability
alone is not a license. The music license above does not cover this effect.

## Metadata inspection

All original 20 music files and `switch.aac` consist of AAC/ADTS frames.
No standalone ID3 or ID3v1 author or license tags were found.
Nineteen original music files contain the encoder string `Lavc61.3.100`.
